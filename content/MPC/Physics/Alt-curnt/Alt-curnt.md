# Ac
I_avg:

$$I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T I(t)\,dt$$
I_avg * t = Q_tot , Iavg is useful for calc tot charge

p_avg:

$$p_{\text{avg}} = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T p(t)\,dt$$
P_avg useful for calc tot energy transferred in time t

I_rms:

$$I_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{T} \int_0^T [I(t)]^2\,dt}$$
I_rms useful for calc power averaged over time period
