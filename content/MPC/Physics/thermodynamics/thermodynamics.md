## Transfer of heat 
### conduction
$$ Q = \frac{kA\Delta T t}{d} $$
resistace :
$$R = \frac{d}{kA}$$

Temperature of junction between two materials 
$$ T_j = \frac{\frac{T_1}{R_{1}} + \frac{T_2}{R_{2}}}{\frac{1}{R_{1}} + \frac{1}{R_{2}}} $$

Newtons cooling :
$$ \frac{T_1 - T_2}{t} = k[ \frac{T_1 + T_2}{2} - T_{0}] $$ 

$T_0$ is surr temp

### radiation
absorptive power = $$ a = \frac{Q_{absorbed}}{Q_{incident}} $$ 
emissive power = $$ E = \frac{Q}{AT}$$ 
energy radiated per unit time per unit area .
emmisivity : $$ e = \frac{E_{object}}{E_{blackbody}} $$   
kirchhoff's law at a given Temperature : $$ \frac{E_{1}}{a_{1}} = E_{blackbody} $$ 
the law holds because at a give temperature , the amount of energy absorbed must equal the amount radiated in thermal equilibrium .  

stefans law : $$ P = e \sigma A T^4 $$ 

wien's displacement law : $$ \lambda_{max} T = b $$ 
where $\lambda_{max}$ is the wavelength with maximum intensity.

mean free path : $$ \lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\pi d^2n}$$
