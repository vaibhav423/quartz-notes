## nickel dimethylglyoxime 

# Key Properties of Dimethylglyoxime (DMG) and its Nickel Complex

### 1. The Reagent: Dimethylglyoxime (DMG)
* **Nature:** It is a bidentate ligand that coordinates through Nitrogen atoms.
* **Role:** Used as a specific reagent for the gravimetric estimation and detection of Nickel ions.

### 2. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
* **Appearance:** The complex formed ($\text{Ni(DMG)}_2$) is a **rosy red** (or strawberry red) precipitate.
* **Reaction Medium:** Precipitation occurs in a **basic medium** (typically using ammonia, $\text{pH} \approx 9$).
* **Solubility:** The complex is **sparingly soluble** in water but dissolves in mineral acids.

### 3. Coordination & Geometry
* **Oxidation State:** Nickel is in the **+2** oxidation state ($\text{Ni}^{2+}$).
* **Hybridization:** The Nickel ion undergoes **$\text{dsp}^2$** hybridization.
* **Geometry:** The complex has a **Square Planar** arrangement.
* **Magnetic Property:** It is **diamagnetic** (zero unpaired electrons) because all eight $3d$ electrons of $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ are paired due to the strong field effect of the ligand.



### 4. Structural Intricacies
* **Chelation:** Each DMG ligand forms one **five-membered chelate ring** with
the Nickel ion. In total, there are **two** such five-membered metal-containing rings.

* **Hydrogen Bonding:** The complex is stabilized by **intramolecular hydrogen bonding** (O-H...O), which leads to the formation of two additional six-membered pseudo-rings.

* **Bond Angles:** The $\text{N-Ni-N}$ bond angle within the chelate ring is slightly
less than, but close to, **$90^\circ$**, consistent with square planar geometry.
