# Easy way : 

# **To maximize $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$  set $\vec{a}$ = $k \vec{b}$**  
# **Example** 

when maximizing $\cos x + \cos y + \cos z$ $= p$ 
under constraint $x+y+z=\pi$       $(1)$
**split p as :**

$\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.$\begin{bmatrix} \cos x \\ \cos y \\ \cos z\end{bmatrix}$ 

**and setting :**

$k$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$=$\begin{bmatrix} \cos x \\ \cos y \\ \cos z\end{bmatrix}$ $\implies$ $\cos x = \cos y = \cos z = k$ 

$\implies x = y = z = \frac{\pi}{3}$  
$\implies p = 3.\left( \cos \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$
$\implies p=\frac{3}{2}$
